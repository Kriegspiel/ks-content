The public website now shares one design across the homepage, rules, blog,
playing guide, leaderboard, and policy pages. This note covers the website
releases `1.5.0` and `1.5.1`; website versions are independent of the app UI
and backend versions.

In website `1.5.0`:

- A new homepage introduces hidden-information chess with an interactive board.
  “Reveal the possibility” shows a legal pair of Black opening moves while
  White stays fixed at e4 and Nf3. Each reveal differs from the previous one.
- The compact header stays visible while the page scrolls. Mobile navigation
  folds into a menu, and the logo switches between light and dark themes.
- Shared typography, spacing, flat controls, and subtle separators give public
  pages a consistent appearance.
- The board keeps the same cool square colors and original black-and-white
  pieces in both page themes.

Website `1.5.1` completes the refinements:

- Blog problem boards use the homepage board palette. Undo, redo, reset, and
  phantom-piece controls follow the same flat styling, with
  larger touch targets.
- Blog posts, rules, privacy, terms, About, and the playing guide use the full
  shared content width, matching the changelog.
- The rules comparison table has a narrower, shaded topic column so it is
  easier to distinguish the topics from the rulesets and fit the columns.
- The header Play link is a dark button with light text in both themes.
- The About version stamp sits between the body text and the footer divider
  with balanced spacing.

The changes preserve the existing problem-board interactions and rules content.
Implementation details are recorded in the
[1.5.0 website change](https://github.com/Kriegspiel/ks-home/pull/218) and
[1.5.1 website refinements](https://github.com/Kriegspiel/ks-home/pull/219).
