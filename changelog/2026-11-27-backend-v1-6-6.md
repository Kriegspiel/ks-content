In all seven rulesets, the referee announces the two directions of a double
check in a fixed order. Rank comes first, followed by file, long diagonal,
short diagonal, and knight. For example, a rank check and a knight check
always appear as rank, then knight. The same order applies to scoresheets
and transcripts for games already in progress.
