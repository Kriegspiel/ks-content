Links to the game app now share the website's updated Kriegspiel preview,
with clear typography, a cool light background, and the familiar hidden board.

The preview uses the homepage's “Chess. Beyond what you can see.” heading.
It is a fixed image for sharing; your selected theme still controls how the
app and its boards appear while playing.

Social platforms may keep an older preview on existing posts until they fetch
the link again.
