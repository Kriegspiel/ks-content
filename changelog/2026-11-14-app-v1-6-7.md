Last-move highlighting now uses a lighter translucent tint. After a move such as
d5xe4, the pawn's former square remains recognizably light instead of looking
like the unhighlighted dark squares beside it. The checker pattern stays visible
whether neighboring squares are highlighted or not.

The app retains its fixed cool board palette and original piece artwork in both
page themes. Selection, legal-move, capture, and illegal-move cues remain visible.
