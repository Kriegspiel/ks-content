Article and problem boards now show the piece following your mouse or touch
while you drag it, matching the feedback in the game app. The original piece
is hidden during the drag so it is clear which piece you are moving.

The board keeps its existing colors, piece artwork, and analysis controls.
Click-to-move, phantom pieces, Undo, Redo, and Reset still work. Dropping a
piece outside the board still removes it as an analysis edit that you can undo;
cancelling a drag leaves the position unchanged.

Try it on the diagrams in
[Problems from La Scacchiera Invisibile](/blog/la-scacchiera-invisibile-problems).
