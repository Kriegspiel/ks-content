Last-move highlighting in the game app now uses a translucent tint over each
square. Light and dark squares remain distinct when both are highlighted, so
adjacent squares after a rook move no longer blend into one solid block.

The board keeps its fixed cool palette and original piece artwork in both page
themes. Selection, legal-move, capture, and illegal-move cues remain visible.
