Dark-mode boards now use warm cream and brown squares across play, review, and Tutor.
The colors match the warm board palette shared by the main website and game app.

Light mode keeps its cool board colors. The original piece artwork and existing
move controls remain unchanged when switching themes.

Last-move highlights retain a light translucent tint, keeping the checker pattern
visible on both the vacated square and the destination.
