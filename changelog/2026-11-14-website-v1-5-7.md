Shared links now use a preview that matches the current Kriegspiel design:
clear typography, a cool light background, and a board with the opponent's
pieces hidden.

The website and game app share the same preview image. It uses the homepage's
“Chess. Beyond what you can see.” heading and the original chess-piece artwork.

Social platforms may keep an older preview on existing posts until they fetch
the link again.
