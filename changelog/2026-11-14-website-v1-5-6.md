The homepage and article boards now share warm cream and brown squares in dark mode.
The colors match the warm board palette shared by the main website and game app.

Light mode keeps its cool board colors. The original piece artwork and existing
move controls remain unchanged when switching themes.

Homepage fog, coordinate labels, and board borders follow the selected board
palette. Revealing a position and moving pieces in article diagrams work as before.
